Apeul — Your A+ Course Companion

A live study platform that turns lecture slides into notes, cheat sheets, and practice questions — with an AI tutor that answers your questions right on the slide.

2026 Live at apeul.com Team project

What it is

Apeul is a study companion my teammates and I have been building and shipping. The idea is simple: your professor's lecture slides are already the best summary of the course — so instead of pasting fragments into a general-purpose chatbot, Apeul works directly on the slides themselves.

A student uploads a lecture deck (PDF or PPTX, up to 100 pages), annotates it the way they would on paper, and generates study material from it. When something doesn't click — say, "why can λ be 0 but v can't?" scribbled next to an eigenvalue slide — the built-in AI tutor answers in the context of that exact slide, with full LaTeX rendering for the math.

Features

  • Annotate directly on slides
    Highlights, text boxes, and handwritten notes — the workflow students already have, kept in one place per course.
  • Generate real study material
    Notes, cheat sheets, and practice questions produced from the actual course content — including questions generated from uploaded past exams.
  • An AI tutor that knows where you are
    Ask about the concept on the slide in front of you and get an explanation grounded in that material, not a generic answer.
  • Built for real coursework
    Course organization with timetable integration, LaTeX formula rendering for quantitative subjects, and private per-user file storage.
