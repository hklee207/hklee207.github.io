I'm a junior studying Industrial & Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan. I moved from Seoul to Toronto when I was six, played on Korea's youth national hockey team, started college and research in Chicago, then served 18 months as a logistics officer and interpreter in the Korean Army. Read the longer story.

Since then I've kept doing one thing: find a slow, manual piece of knowledge work and build a tool that makes it fast. At GLG Korea that was a value-chain research tool for the business development team. With friends it's Apeul, a study companion for college courses.

Every week my dad sends me a report on the world economy, and every trading day a short market note. Talking those through with him is how I learn to see the world. I write down what I take from it in Worldview.

Now

  • Researching learning to defer with Prof. Wentao Weng at U-M IOE: when should an AI decide on its own, and when should it hand a task to a human expert with limited capacity?
  • Looking for a Summer 2027 role at an early-stage AI startup, working on growth and go-to-market.
  • Building Apeul with my team, and small tools for myself, like a job agent that reads application portals so I don't have to.
  • Going back to fundamentals: math, philosophy, psychology.

Projects

  • Value Chain Explorer ↗

    Type a company name and get its value chain, impact-scored news pinned to each link, and the experts to call about each signal. English and Korean. Built solo at GLG Korea, 2026. Write-up.

    Access code on the write-up page.

  • Apeul ↗

    Your A+ course companion: lecture slides become notes, cheat sheets and practice questions, with an AI tutor in context. Built with my team. Write-up.

  • Hypothesis CDD Predictor ↗

    A Flask + Claude app that drafts a MECE hypothesis tree for commercial due diligence. Open source. Write-up.

  • Job Agent 27 ↗

    My personal Summer 2027 job agent. Three times a day it pulls new postings from LinkedIn and Jobright, drops reposts, reads each real application page for work-authorization language, and sends me a digest. The dashboard updates three times a day.

  • Tinkerly ↗

    A newspaper I built from my dad's weekly world reports and daily market notes: a front page, a timeline and map of how the world changed, and an analyst agent that writes down forecasts and scores them. My own takeaways live in Worldview.

  • S&P 500 Top 20 Dashboard

    DCF, DDM and multiples for the twenty largest S&P 500 names, with a Monte Carlo risk model. Notebook.

  • Gold Monte Carlo

    Simulating gold price paths, then adjusting them for Fed policy. Notebooks: baseline, Fed-adjusted. Plus a live gold price tracker.
